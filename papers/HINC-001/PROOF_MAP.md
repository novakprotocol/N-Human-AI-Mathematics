# HINC-001 Proof Map

This map identifies load-bearing dependencies so a reviewer can audit the paper without reconstructing its logical structure from prose.

## Common foundation

```text
Hochschild Gerstenhaber algebra of dual numbers in characteristic two
        ↓
explicit subalgebras E and O inside D[u]
        ↓
finite homogeneous generators, algebra relations, and bracket relations
        ↓
coefficient equations for strict graded endomorphisms
```

The MCRC family is not required for this standalone chain. It is an application after the classification.

## Even classification chain

### E0 — Explicit presentation

```text
E = F + uD[u] = F[r,s]/(s^2)
|r|=|s|=1
[r,s]=r
```

Audit tasks:

- verify the identification inside `D[u]`;
- verify all displayed brackets;
- verify no additional relation is used implicitly.

### E1 — General graded degree-one map

Because `E_1` is free on `r,s`, write:

```text
r -> lambda r + beta s
s -> eta r + delta s.
```

### E2 — Algebra relation

Preserving `s^2=0` gives `eta^2=0` in characteristic two.

Dependency: `r^2` must span a free `R`-summand over arbitrary commutative base algebra `R`.

### E3 — Bracket relation

Preserving `[r,s]=r` gives:

```text
beta = 0
lambda(delta - 1) = 0.
```

### E4 — Sufficiency

Show the normal-form assignment preserves:

- `s^2=0`;
- `[r,s]=r`;
- all remaining generator brackets;
- all algebra products generated by `r,s`.

This generator-to-global step is load bearing. The current expanded Lean source formalizes coefficient equivalence and monoid closure but not the entire generated Gerstenhaber-algebra preservation theorem.

### E5 — Composition

Direct substitution gives:

```text
(lambda,delta,eta)(lambda',delta',eta')
 = (lambda lambda', delta delta', lambda eta' + delta' eta).
```

### E6 — Representability

The coefficient equations define:

```text
A_E = F[lambda,delta,eta]/(eta^2,lambda(delta-1)).
```

The composition polynomials induce the bialgebra comultiplication.

### E7 — Geometry

From `A_E`:

- nilradical generated by `eta`;
- reduction `F[lambda,delta]/(lambda(delta-1))`;
- two crossing affine lines;
- connected, one-dimensional underlying scheme;
- explicit noncommutative dual-number witness.

### E8 — Units and commutators

Invertibility of the degree-one matrix forces `lambda` and `delta` units; the crossing relation forces `delta=1`. Derive the affine group law and commutator formula.

### E9 — Center and derived group

Use universal commutation against arbitrary test-ring points. This step must be scheme-theoretic, not only field-point testing.

Dependencies:

- complete coefficient comparison;
- nonreduced test algebras;
- fppf/image interpretation for the derived subgroup.

## Odd classification chain

### O0 — Explicit presentation

Define generators:

```text
epsilon, a=epsilon u, b=u^2, c=u^3
```

with the displayed algebra relations and Gerstenhaber brackets.

### O1 — General homogeneous map

Write the most general degree-compatible images with coefficients:

```text
x, lambda, mu, p, q, rho, sigma.
```

Audit that the selected bases span the relevant homogeneous pieces over arbitrary `R`.

### O2 — Eliminate off-diagonal coefficients

Use algebra and bracket relations to derive:

```text
x = q = sigma = 0.
```

### O3 — Diagonal equations

Derive:

```text
p = lambda rho
rho = lambda p^2
lambda(mu - 1) = 0
rho(mu - 1) = 0.
```

The odd arbitrary-base elimination is one of the highest-priority external review points.

### O4 — Sufficiency

Verify the diagonal assignment preserves every defining product and bracket. As in the even case, this generator-to-global step remains outside the current coefficient-level Lean file.

### O5 — Composition and representability

Composition is coordinatewise on normalized data. Derive the representing reduced affine monoid scheme.

### O6 — Clopen split

Identify the group-like idempotent separating the additional unit component from the crossing component. Verify the decomposition is functorial and scheme-theoretic.

## Common-core synthesis

```text
Even reduction = crossing C
Odd nonunit/core component = crossing C
        ↓
common reduced monoid core
```

Then compare the modifications:

```text
Even: C with square-zero skew direction
Odd:  C plus separate clopen unit component
```

## Secondary consequences

The following depend on the completed monoid classifications:

- idempotent schemes;
- center descriptions;
- tangent spaces;
- normalization;
- point counts;
- zeta functions;
- MCRC parity application.

A reviewer finding a flaw in E4 or O4 should treat downstream consequences as blocked until repaired.
