The surrounding theories are established. The current candidate contribution is the explicit paired classification and its common-core interpretation—not the invention of algebraic monoids, functors of points, Hochschild cohomology, Gerstenhaber brackets, dual numbers, nilpotent group schemes, or scheme-theoretic automorphisms.

No exact indexed occurrence of the complete theorem package was located in the project's targeted search. That negative search does **not** establish historical absence or worldwide priority.

## Established frameworks

### Algebraic monoids and algebraic semigroups

General structure theory already studies affine algebraic monoids, open unit groups, idempotents, connected components, and centers. These tools supply the language in which the HINC monoids are described.

### Endomorphism monoids

General realization results show that broad classes of affine algebraic monoids can occur as endomorphism monoids of finite-dimensional algebraic objects. Therefore, merely producing a nonreduced, disconnected, or noncommutative endomorphism monoid is not a novelty claim.

### Scheme-valued automorphisms and gradings

Functor-of-points and group-scheme methods for graded algebraic structures are established. The need to test arbitrary commutative base algebras rather than only fields is standard scheme-theoretic discipline.

### Hochschild and Gerstenhaber theory

The Hochschild cohomology of the dual numbers, Gerstenhaber operations, deformation-theoretic interpretations, invariance questions, and structural maps related to ideals and equivalences all have substantial existing literatures.

### Nonreduced group schemes

The group scheme `alpha_2`, square-zero directions, semidirect products, fppf sheaves, and infinitesimal centers are established concepts.

## Closest known mechanisms

The existing literature appears to contain the component mechanisms separately:

- algebraic monoid structure and unit-group theory;
- endomorphism-monoid realization;
- automorphism group schemes of graded structures;
- Hochschild/Gerstenhaber structure and functoriality;
- dual-number and nilpotent deformation calculations.

The unresolved question is whether a general theorem, after translation, immediately yields the two complete classifications and common-core description.

## Candidate project-specific contribution

The strongest defensible candidate contribution is:

> An explicit arbitrary-base classification of the strict graded Gerstenhaber endomorphism monoids of two specified subalgebras of `HH*(F[epsilon]/epsilon^2)` in characteristic two, together with a common crossing-core theorem and a contrast between infinitesimal skew thickening and clopen unit doubling.

A secondary conceptual contribution is the fully explicit hidden-center example:

> Every ordinary field-valued unit may appear central while the universal group scheme has trivial center and nontrivial infinitesimal derived subgroup.
