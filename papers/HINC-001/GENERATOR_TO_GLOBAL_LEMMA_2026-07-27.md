## 1. Exact structure

For this note, a **binary Gerstenhaber algebra** is a graded-commutative unital algebra `A` together with a degree-minus-one bracket satisfying the graded Lie identities and the biderivation rule.

HINC-001 classifies unital, base-linear, degree-preserving maps that preserve the product and this binary bracket. It does not claim preservation of a BV operator, restricted power, brace algebra, or full `E_2` structure.

## 2. General lemma

Let `A` be a binary Gerstenhaber algebra generated as a unital algebra by homogeneous elements

```text
g_1,...,g_m.
```

Let `B` be another binary Gerstenhaber algebra, and let

```text
phi:A -> B
```

be a unital graded algebra homomorphism.

Assume that for every pair of generators,

```text
phi([g_i,g_j]) = [phi(g_i),phi(g_j)].
```

Then `phi` preserves the bracket on all of `A`.

## 3. Proof

The Gerstenhaber bracket is a derivation in each argument. For homogeneous elements, one may write the two Leibniz identities in the chosen sign convention as

```text
[x,yz] = [x,y]z + sign(x,y) y[x,z],
[xy,z] = x[y,z] + sign(y,z) [x,z]y.
```

The exact signs are immaterial to the induction provided the same convention is used in `A` and `B`; in characteristic two all signs evaluate to `+1` in the HINC applications.

Proceed by induction on the total algebra-word length of a pair of monomials.

- Length two is the assumed generator-pair table.
- If the second monomial is a product `yz`, expand `[x,yz]` by the first Leibniz identity. Apply the induction hypothesis to `[x,y]` and `[x,z]`, then use multiplicativity of `phi`.
- If the first monomial is a product, apply the second Leibniz identity and the same argument.
- Extend by base-linearity to sums.

Thus

```text
phi([a,b]) = [phi(a),phi(b)]
```

for all `a,b in A`.

## 4. Quotient-presentation condition

The lemma applies to a presented algebra only after two separate facts are established:

1. the proposed generator images satisfy every defining algebra relation, so the algebra homomorphism exists;
2. the displayed relations give a complete presentation of the source algebra.

The first condition is exactly the coefficient-elimination calculation. The second condition is immediate for the even presentation and is supplied for the odd presentation in `ODD_PRESENTATION_NORMAL_FORM_2026-07-27.md`.

## 5. Application to the even algebra

The even algebra is generated by

```text
r=u,
s=epsilon u
```

with relation `s^2=0` and generator brackets

```text
[r,s]=r,
[r,r]=[s,s]=0.
```

A unital graded algebra map satisfying the relation and this bracket table therefore preserves the binary bracket globally.

The coefficient equations reduce exactly to

```text
beta=0,
eta^2=0,
lambda(delta-1)=0.
```

## 6. Application to the odd algebra

The odd algebra is generated by

```text
epsilon,
a=epsilon u,
b=u^2,
c=u^3.
```

Once the algebra relations and the complete generator bracket table are preserved, the lemma gives global binary-bracket preservation.

The normal-form proof for the algebra presentation is supplied separately. The coefficient equations reduce to

```text
x=q=sigma=0,
p=lambda rho,
rho=lambda p^2,
lambda(mu-1)=0,
rho(mu-1)=0.
```
