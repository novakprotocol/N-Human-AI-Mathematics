# Human–AI mathematics landscape for ABF-001

## Direct answer

ABF-001 is not the first human–AI mathematics project, the first AI-assisted theorem, or the first AI-assisted mathematical discovery. Public precedents are substantial.

## Representative public precedents

- **GPT-f / Metamath (2020).** A transformer-based prover found shorter formal proofs that were accepted into the Metamath library.
- **AlphaTensor (2022).** DeepMind used reinforcement learning to discover matrix-multiplication algorithms.
- **FunSearch (2023).** An LLM plus automated evaluator found new cap-set constructions and bin-packing heuristics, with mathematician Jordan Ellenberg collaborating on the cap-set work.
- **AlphaDev (2023).** Reinforcement learning discovered improved sorting and hashing algorithms, some incorporated into widely used libraries.
- **AlphaGeometry and AlphaProof (2024).** Neurosymbolic and formal systems achieved strong Olympiad-level mathematical performance.
- **AlphaEvolve (2025–2026).** LLM-guided evolutionary program search reported improvements on open mathematical and algorithmic problems.
- **Agentic Neurosymbolic Collaboration (2026).** Human strategic direction, LLM agents, symbolic tools, exhaustive enumeration, and Lean verification jointly produced a new combinatorial-design bound.
- **Human-in-the-loop formalization and mathematical workbenches.** Systems such as Lean-oriented proof assistants, MathCoPilot-like environments, and agentic formalization pipelines explicitly organize human and model collaboration.

## What may be unusual here

The potentially differentiating feature is not that a human and an LLM worked on mathematics. It is the integrated public evidence and governance model:

- multiple independently bounded paper packages rather than one demonstration;
- complete human proof maps separated from computational evidence;
- exact finite exhaustive checks and negative controls;
- hostile review that must reach zero unresolved blockers;
- explicit prior-art and equivalence ledgers with same-day deltas;
- preserved failed attempts and corrections;
- exact source commits, manifests, tags, releases, assets, and provenance attestations;
- machine-enforced claim vocabulary and release gates;
- structured public channels for counterexamples, proof gaps, equivalence reports, reproduction, and formalization.

A systematic search found no exact indexed match for this entire combined publication architecture. That observation does not establish that the architecture is historically first or unique.

## Public wording rule

Authorized:

> N Human–LLM Mathematics is a human-led, LLM-assisted, evidence-controlled public technical-review program for candidate mathematics.

Not authorized:

- first human–AI mathematical discovery;
- first human–AI mathematics collaboration;
- first AI-authored theorem;
- globally unique publication model;
- peer reviewed, externally reproduced, or historically first without separate evidence.
