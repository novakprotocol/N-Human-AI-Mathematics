First candidate package

HINC-001

Candidate technical review

Hidden Infinitesimal Noncommutativity

The manuscript classifies unital, base-linear, degree-preserving endomorphisms preserving cup product and the binary Gerstenhaber bracket for two explicit characteristic-two subalgebras of the Hochschild cohomology of the dual numbers.

The even reduction and odd nonunit component share the crossing C = Spec F[x,y]/(x(y−1)); the even object adds a constrained skew square-zero direction, while the odd object adds a separate clopen torus unit component.

Second candidate package

ABF-001

Candidate technical review · active

Affine Restriction Moment Kernels

The manuscript proves a Reed–Muller moment criterion for affine restriction degree drops and classifies the complete radical-incidence geometry of one specified map from GF(2)16 to GF(2)8.

Exact finite record: 130,559 degree-15 restrictions, 511 degree-14 restrictions, no lower vectorial restrictions; 203 singular mask-indexed radicals, 202 distinct subspaces, 469 incidences, 467 affine parameters, and 201 forest components.
