HINC-001

Private hold · consolidated rewrite pending

Hidden Infinitesimal Noncommutativity

The candidate theorem classifies unital, base-linear, degree-preserving endomorphisms preserving cup product and the binary degree-minus-one Gerstenhaber bracket for two explicit subalgebras of the dual-number Hochschild algebra.

Candidate synthesis: the even reduction and odd nonunit component share the crossing C = Spec F[x,y]/(x(y−1)); the even object adds a skew square-zero direction, while the odd object adds a separate clopen torus unit component.
