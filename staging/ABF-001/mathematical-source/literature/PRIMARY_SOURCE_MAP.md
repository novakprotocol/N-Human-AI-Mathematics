# ABF-001 primary-source map

## Closest affine-restriction literature

1. Claude Carlet, Serge Feukoua, and Ana Sălăgean, *The stability of the algebraic degree of Boolean functions when restricted to affine spaces*, Designs, Codes and Cryptography 93 (2025), 4799-4832, DOI `10.1007/s10623-025-01702-z`, arXiv `2409.20211`.
   - Establishes degree-drop affine spaces, stability, structural characterizations, counting, and complete small-variable classifications.
   - Closest scalar restriction theory.
   - Does not state the ABF two-sided vector-moment incidence theorem or the frozen numerical packet.

2. Claude Carlet, Serge Feukoua, and Ana Sălăgean, *On the algebraic degree stability of vectorial Boolean functions when restricted to affine subspaces*, arXiv `2504.03307`.
   - Closest vectorial degree-stability theory.
   - Treats power functions, codimension conditions, inverse/Kasami functions, quadratic APN functions, and counting.
   - Does not give the ABF moment pencil or finite radical forest.

3. Christof Beierle, Gregor Leander, and Léo Perrin, *Trims and extensions of quadratic APN functions*, Designs, Codes and Cryptography 90 (2022), 1009-1036, DOI `10.1007/s10623-022-01024-4`, arXiv `2108.13280`; supplementary software DOI `10.5281/zenodo.5871840`.
   - Restricts vectorial functions to affine hyperplanes and projects outputs, producing EA-invariant trim spectra.
   - Close application framework but a different invariant and different function class.

4. Philipp Heering, Christian Kaspers, and Vladislav Taranchuk, *On Reed-Muller subcodes, Grassmannian partitions and sum-free functions*, arXiv `2605.22958` (2026).
   - Connects affine-subspace sums of vectorial functions to Reed-Muller subcodes.
   - Important recent overlap with the moment/sum language.
   - Does not state the ABF mask/hyperplane bidual kernel or frozen rank-incidence classification.

5. Serge Christian Feukoua Jonzo, Ana Sălăgean, and Pantelimon Stănică, *The nonlinearity of restrictions of direct sums of monomials and of threshold functions*, Designs, Codes and Cryptography, online 25 July 2026.
   - Same-day-nearby restriction literature focused on nonlinearity rather than ABF's moment degree certificate.
   - Included in the final delta watch because it is newly published.

## Closest symmetric-form literature

6. Rod Gow, *Dimension bounds for constant rank subspaces of symmetric bilinear forms over a finite field*, arXiv `1502.05547`.
7. Rod Gow, *Rank-related dimension bounds for subspaces of symmetric bilinear forms*, arXiv `1602.03077`.
8. Rod Gow, *Rank-related dimension bounds for subspaces of bilinear forms over finite fields*, arXiv `1703.07266`.

These papers establish the general rank/radical landscape. They do not identify ABF's eight-dimensional 17-by-17 binary pencil, exact rank histogram, duplicate radical line, or 201-component incidence forest.

## Other discovery surfaces

- IACR ePrint `2005/025`, *Analysis of Affinely Equivalent Boolean Functions*, was reviewed as an affine-invariant/classification analogue, not an exact match.
- Claude Carlet's *Boolean Functions for Cryptography and Coding Theory* (2021) and Lilya Budaghyan's *Construction and Analysis of Cryptographic Functions* (2015) were checked as book-level terminology and equivalence references.
- HAL, Zenodo, thesis, GitHub, and public mathematical-index searches produced no exact ABF match in the recorded query families.
